President docs · Training calendar

Eight sessions, twice weekly, during lunch or after school · October 6–November 1

Week 1

Session 1 · Welcome + role assignment

Walk through the constitution and each officer’s role expectations document together.

Session 2 · Foundations

Discuss EQ foundations and Goleman basics, then walk through the full curriculum so everyone sees the whole arc before owning a piece of it.

Week 2

Session 3 · Experience the meeting

Run a live walkthrough of the 40-minute meeting template. Officers participate as students instead of only hearing how it works.

Session 4 · Assign curriculum ownership

Give each officer two or three meetings to own, plus matched reading from Cialdini, Voss, Rosenberg, or Gallo according to role.

Attend the CHS Application Info Meeting whenever it is scheduled this week or next. Once CHS sets the date, treat it as fixed and non-negotiable.

Week 3

Session 5 · Practice-teach

Each officer teaches their assigned meeting to the rest of the team and receives real feedback.

Session 6 · Application work session

Finalize the constitution, semester plan, and officer-centered answers together as a team.

Week 4

Session 7 · Mock interview

Practice for CHS’s later interview round in mid-November and sharpen how officers explain the club generally.

Session 8 · Dress rehearsal + submit

Run a full dress rehearsal of Meeting 1, then submit the club application.
