Officer applications for the founding team.

SICA launches with five officers. This is the team that writes the first curriculum, runs the first meetings, and sets what the club becomes — before there is anything to inherit.

The five roles

Filled

President

Sets the curriculum, teaches the idea each week, holds the clock. Currently Reyansh Nankani.

Open

Vice President

Runs the practice activity, pairs people up, watches for anyone left out. Teaches a full session by week four.

Open

Treasurer + Secretary

Attendance, minutes, and the club’s money. Writes what happened into the record within 48 hours.

Open

Public Relations

Announcements, recruitment, and everything SICA says publicly.

Open

Media Director

Photos and video during meetings — with consent, and never during a practice activity.

What we’re selecting for

  1. Follow-through over charisma

    A quiet officer who delivers beats a compelling one who doesn’t. There’s a short trial task before any decision.

  2. Warmth toward beginners

    The test is simple: would the most nervous person in the room approach you?

  3. About 45 minutes a week

    Outside the meeting itself. Deliberately small enough to survive AP season.
