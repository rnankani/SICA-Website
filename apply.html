Apply for a founding officer role.

SICA launches with five officers. This is the team that writes the first curriculum, runs the first meetings, and decides what the club becomes — before there is anything to inherit. About 45 minutes a week outside the meeting.

Which role

About you

Three questions

Short answers are fine — two or three sentences each. We are reading for how you think, not how much you write.

The most useful answer on this page. Everyone has one — saying so early is the point.

What happens next

Two officers read every application. If it looks like a fit you get a fifteen-minute conversation, then one real piece of work with a week to do it — a run of show, a week’s discussion questions, a flyer. We decide within three days of that and tell everyone either way, including the people we don’t pick.
