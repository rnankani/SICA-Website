The time commitment changes by phase

  • Right now, before approval: minimal — mostly showing up to training sessions.
  • During training, October 6–November 1: about 1–1.5 hours a week across two sessions, plus prep for meetings you are assigned to own.
  • Once SICA is approved and running: Vice President about 2 hours a week; Treasurer/Secretary and PR/Media about 1–2 hours a week.

The interview

A relaxed 15-minute, one-on-one conversation with Reyansh, SICA’s founder. No panel and no presentation.

Three open seats

One Vice President, one Treasurer/Secretary, and one PR/Media Director. A strong runner-up may be offered another open role; otherwise, they can join the waitlist.

Who can apply

Any grade can apply for these officer roles. Only the President role has the CHS sophomore/junior restriction, and that role is not currently open.

If you miss training

Each session is summarized. Missing one occasionally is fine; missing most sessions is not.
