Apply for a founding officer role.

SICA launches with five officers. This is the team that writes the first curriculum, runs the first meetings, and decides what the club becomes — before there is anything to inherit. About 45 minutes a week outside the meeting.

1Basic info

LinkedIn, a personal site, a resume, Instagram — anything that shows what you have made.

2About you

Under 400 words each. We are reading for how you think, not how much you write.

Specific and recent. “I am a perfectionist” is not an answer.

3About SICA

Your own read on it, not the tagline.

4Availability & bandwidth

Context, not a disqualifier. It helps us plan around real bandwidth.

5Anything else

What happens next

The founder reads every application. If it looks like a fit you get a short conversation, then one real piece of work with a week to do it — a run of show, a week’s discussion questions, a flyer. We decide within three days of that and tell everyone either way, including the people we don’t pick.
