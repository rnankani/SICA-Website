How access works

Nobody outside this list can create an account — the database refuses the sign-up, not just the page. When someone on the list registers, their row moves to Registered and they land as a member, which opens nothing. Approve is what grants the role and lets them into the workspace. Deny or Remove puts them straight back to member.
