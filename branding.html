Voice

Do
  • Write for a 9th grader with zero context.
  • Short sentences. Concrete examples.
  • Use only numbers you can source.
  • Stay calm — restraint is the trust signal.
Don’t
  • “Executive presence”, “command a room”, “read the room”.
  • Exclamation marks or hype.
  • Countdowns, “limited spots”, urgency.
  • Invented statistics of any kind.

Logo rules

Do
  • Solid brass on near-black, or bone on brass.
  • Keep clear space of one “S” width on every side.
  • Always set in Bodoni Moda 700.
Don’t
  • Gradients or metallic effects on the letters.
  • Outlines, shadows, or stretching.
  • Any colour other than brass or bone.

Who may publish under these rules is set in Handbook §7.
